**2.25**

## New

- Search by what's in a picture. On Macs with Apple Intelligence on macOS 27, Folio has your Mac look at each picture and note what it sees, then searches that too: "pill shapes", "cheerful" or "people at a table" find pictures nobody tagged. It happens on your Mac, in the background, and the details panel shows what your Mac saw. Settings ▸ Tagging can turn it off.

## Improvements

- Libraries open in this version are updated to hold the descriptions; Folio keeps a backup of the library from before.
